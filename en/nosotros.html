02 — Our story

Manufacturing, distribution and maintenance of medical equipment

Founded in 1978 by Eng. Roberto Bonilla Fernández, FEHLMEX was born with a clear purpose: to manufacture, distribute and maintain medical equipment with Mexican quality.

More than 40 years later, we remain a 100% Mexican company, established as a provider of technology solutions for the healthcare and life sciences sector: hospitals, laboratories, clinics, the medical industry, manufacturing, pharmaceuticals and agriculture.
